# Plan Structure (canonical — from coach's "Paolo Plan" PDF)

_This is the ultimate template for structuring every weekly calendar. Source: "Paolo Plan.pdf" (received via Messages, adopted 2026-07-05)._

## How the planner applies this plan

- The workout order is tracked in the database: the profile's "Workout rotation" gives the next workouts and the last prescription of each one. A workout's `workoutType` names it: `ride-threshold-a`, `run-vo2-b`, and so on.
- Paolo's change: **never a heavy lift on the same day as a workout.** The heavy lift goes on an aerobic day.
- Paolo's change (2026-10-24): **one session a day, or one stacked block.** A lift on an aerobic day follows the
  easy aerobic part straight away (an easy run to the gym, or Zwift then the home lift), never hours later and never
  followed by a run. coaching.md has the rule and the floor-week and monthly rules.
- The goals (Coach → Settings → Goals) choose bike or run: at least 1 run workout each week for the 10k goal; rides for VO2 max and ride km.
- A 10k test run at goal pace (`run-test-10k`) replaces one workout about every 6 weeks. It does not move the order.
- From November to June, a ski tour (`ski-tour`) can replace the long ride as the weekend long aerobic day.

## Weekday Schedule

| Day | Type |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Workout |
| Day 2 | Aerobic |
| Day 3 | Workout |
| Day 4 | Aerobic/recovery |
| Day 5 | Workout |

- Adjust as needed; if only one weekday is available, make it a workout.
- After a workout day, next day is aerobic or recovery — goal is to be recovered for the next workout.
- Lifts ideally go on workout days **after** the workout; can go any day, just never back-to-back. (Paolo's changes above win: heavy lifts on aerobic days, stacked right after the easy part.)

## Weekend Schedule

- Weekends = long run (with workout) + long bike. Either day order.
- If the long workout lands on Sunday, start the next week with an aerobic recovery day.
- Long rides: 2–6 h, mostly Z1/Z2. May incorporate a workout **only if the rest of the week was light**.
- Long runs: 90 min–3 h, mostly Z1, can have a solid Z2 component.
- Progress by adding time and lengthening time at the high end of Z2.

## Workout Rotation (cycle through in order; each has its own progression)

| # | Workout |
|---|---|
| 1 | Threshold A |
| 2 | VO2 A |
| 3 | VO2 B |
| 4 | Threshold B |
| 5 | Threshold A |
| 6 | VO2 A |

Progress each workout's own progression only if you hit the last one; if you failed, regress or repeat.

Focus: threshold + VO2 max, tempo as support, top-end speed as sprints on aerobic days + occasional sprint workouts.

On easy/long days: 6 × 6–8 s sprints (bike) or 4–6 × 20 s hard strides (run), ≥3 min full rest between (neuromuscular).

Zones: prescribe by HR for all but Z5 (HR needs ~5 min at wattage to settle); calibrate pace zones off HR; go by feel too. Z2 = strong but sustainable for many hours. Z4 = ~1 h sustainable. VO2 = 4–6 min sustainable.

## Workout Bank (bike; run mods noted)

### Threshold A
- 30 min warm up
- 6 × 5 min @ top end of Z4; 2 min recovery (start reasonable, end with a few left in tank)
- 10–20 min top end of Z2 (lactate clearance)
- Easy til end — 1 to 2.5 h total
- **Progression**: add sets up to 10 × 5
- Run: 10 min WU, max 90 min

### Threshold B
- 20 min warm up
- 5 × [1' Z4; 1' Z5 (~15–20 W above previous minute); 1' Z4; 1' Z5; 1' Z4; 3' easy]
- 10–20 min top end of Z2
- Easy til end — 1 to 2.5 h total
- **Progression**: add 1 min to a Z4 segment each time until set length = 8 min
- Run: 5 × [1' hard; 2' Z4; 3' easy]; max 1 h

### VO2 A
- 15 min easy warm up
- 4 × 3' hard; 3' easy (best sustainable — highest wattage maintainable across whole workout)
- 10–20 min top end of Z2 (**do not drop from this one**)
- Easy til end — 1 to 2.5 h total
- **Progression**: build sets to 5, then build reps (work + rest) by 30 s to 5 × 5
- Run: same; max 1 h

### VO2 B
- 15 min easy warm up
- 3 × [6 × 30 s hard / 30 s easy]; 5' easy between sets (best sustainable)
- 10–20 min top end of Z2
- Easy til end — 1 to 2.5 h total
- **Progression**: build each set to 10 (3 × [10 × 30/30]), then lengthen sets
- Run: 12 × 1' hard / 1' easy; max 1 h → build to 15 × 1/1, then decrease recovery

### Tempo (support)
- 15 min WU; 3 × 20' Z3 low cadence (60–70 rpm); 5' easy; 15' CD or extend
- **Progression**: add 5 min per interval
- Run: same, can shorten intervals

### Aerobic days
- 30–90 min Z1/Z2 (bike mostly Z2; run Z1/low Z2) + strides/sprints. Recover for the next workout.

### Gym Lift (heavy)
- Warm up circuit × 3: 15 stair climbers, 5 air squats, 5 burpees, 10 split jumps, 60 s rest
- 3 × 5: squat, Bulgarian split squats, single-leg RDL, weighted calf raises, [optional] pullups
- 3–5 reps, work to 3 × 5 at a weight then add weight, 3–5 min rest
- Short on time: squat + calf raise + curls only
- Curls every lift (Paolo, 2026-11-02): see "Curls in every lift" below.

### Curls in every lift (Paolo, 2026-11-02)

Every lift has a biceps curl: dumbbell curl 3 × 10–12, slow 3 s down (one exercise in every template, so its
history and progression are shared). The Lift templates carry it, so a gym lift gets it from its template. A shortened lift
keeps its curls, and so does a home lift in place of a gym one (the Arm finisher's dumbbell curl counts).

### Hotel Lift (power focus)
- ~50% 1RM, explosive intent, 60–90 s rest, not to failure
- Same warm-up circuit × 3
- Explosive: box split jump 3 × 5, box jump from seated × 3 (unweighted)
- Main: hamstring curl (ball) or single-leg DB RDL 3 × 8; pistol squat 3 × 8 (band-assisted OK); single-leg calf raise 3 × 8
- Dumbbell curl 3 × 10–12

### Home mini sessions (no gym; from the coach's TrainingPeaks library)

Short bodyweight / dumbbell sessions for home: prehab for the knees and Achilles, mobility after long days, and the
strength floor on travel or newborn weeks. They are not a third lift: use one on an aerobic or rest day, or in place of
a lift when the week is short. No Lift template: the exercise lines below ARE the session. Write them into the
workout's `description` exactly in this form (`1) Name 3x8: cue`), one per line, so Coach can make the Lift session
from them and every set can be logged (web "Log sets", phone Start workout). Holds and foam rolling stay plain lines
(no `NxM`), so they are not counted as sets. `activityType` strength; `workoutType` `strength-home` or `mobility`.

### Physio (right Achilles, right knee and hip): 1–2 a week, every week

Rehab for his right Achilles, right knee and hip, built from the home programs his physios and his coach gave him
(2020–2024: heavy slow calf raises, front-of-knee work at a slow tempo, single-leg control and hip strength). The
clinic's latest sheets were given in person, so update these when he has a new one.

- **How many.** Two a week on a full week (Physio A and Physio B, at least 48 h apart); one on a floor week, travel or
  newborn week (Physio A: 20 min, a step and a dumbbell, works in a hotel). They are not a lift and not a home mini
  session: plan them on top of the 2 lifts.
- **Where.** A rest day or an easy day on its own, or as one block straight after a ride (the bike barely loads the
  Achilles; the one-session-a-day rule holds, so never hours apart from another session). Not on a lift day (the lift
  loads the same legs) and never before a run. Physio A, the heavy calf work, not the day before a run workout or the
  long run; the day after the long run is good. 15–25 min.
- **Format.** As the home mini sessions: `activityType` strength, `workoutType` `strength-home`, the title starts with
  "🩹 Physio" (the plan check counts sessions by that word), the numbered lines copied into `description` as written.
  The names are the bodyweight ones below, not the gym's ("Standing calf raise" is the calf machine in Lift), so these
  sets keep their own history.
- **Load.** Tendon rehab is slow and heavy: add weight (a dumbbell in the hand on that side) when the last 2 reps of a
  set stop being hard. Achilles pain up to 3/10 during the set that settles by the next morning is fine; worse or
  stiffer the next morning, go back to the last load and skip the run the day after. Right side first; when one side
  is weaker, the weak side sets the reps.

**Physio A — Achilles & calf** (20 min; the coach's Achilles work plus the physio's heel and foot work).
Calf foam roll 2–3 min: find a sore spot, stay on it and move the ankle around.
1) Single-leg bent-knee calf raise 2x8: slow, 2–3 s up and 2–3 s down, off a step; a dumbbell so the last reps are hard; 30–60 s rest or alternate legs.
2) Single-leg straight-knee calf raise 2x8: same tempo, off a step, knee locked.
3) Wall drill heel raise 3x8: each side; hands on the wall, drive the knee forward and rise onto the big toe.
4) Mid-foot bridge tap 3x15: bridge up on the mid-foot, tap one heel down, then the other.
Long-lever hamstring bridge hold: 20 s each side, 3 rounds (25 s from week 2). The marker is a heel-off bridge for 60 s with no cramp.

**Physio B — Knee & hip** (25 min; the physio's knee strength session at the coach's front-of-knee tempo, and the
physio's hip work).
1) Single-leg step down 3x8: each side, off a step, 2–3 s down; knee over the second toe, hips level; right leg first.
2) Bodyweight split squat 3x8: each side, 2–3 s down and up; hold a dumbbell when 8 feel easy.
3) Side-lying hip abduction 3x8: each side; roll the hips forward, top leg slightly back (toes out of sight); the burn is in the back pocket, not the front of the thigh.
4) Standing wall clam 3x12: each side; foot 2 ft from the wall, weight in the standing heel, hinge at the hips, don't lean on the wall.
5) Single-leg Romanian deadlift 3x10: each leg, 15–20 lb; hips level, standing knee soft.
6) Single-leg bent-knee calf raise 2x8: as in Physio A, slow.
Runner's split squat hold: 20 s each side, 3 rounds.
Adductor squeeze hold: a ball or pillow between the knees on a chair, 15–20 s, 3 rounds.

### Back & hip stretch: every day for now (from 2026-10-26)

Paolo asked for more stretching (2026-10-26). A gentle 10 min routine for the low back, hip flexors and glutes, every
day until he says to stop; the coach checks with him on 2026-11-16.

- **Where.** Straight after the day's session as one block (after the physio on a physio day), or alone on a rest
  day. Never hours apart from another session.
- **Format.** `activityType` strength, `workoutType` `mobility`, title "🧘 Back & hip stretch", the lines below copied
  into `description`. The holds stay plain lines, so only the moving drills are counted as sets.
- **Feel.** A stretch, never pain: mild tension that eases as he holds it. Anything sharp: stop that one.

**Back & hip stretch** (10 min)
1) Cat-cow 1x10: slow, breathe out as the back rounds.
2) Glute bridge 2x10: squeeze the glutes, ribs down, no arch in the low back.
3) Bird dog 2x8: each side, 2 s hold, hips level.
4) 90-90 1x10: hip switches, slow.
Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch: 45 s each side, 2 rounds; tuck the pelvis and squeeze the glute of the back leg (the stretch is high in the front of the hip).
Figure-4 glute stretch, lying on the back: 45 s each side, 2 rounds.
Child's pose side reach: 30 s each side, walk both hands to one side (the stretch is along the side of the low back).
Knees to chest: 30 s, rock gently.
