## Criteria

- Open gym only, where you walk in and lift on your own. No class-based studios.
- About a 20-minute walk from home (~1.6 km).
- Must handle the arm days. That means racks, adjustable benches, dumbbells, an EZ bar, a preacher bench, a dip station, a chin-up bar and a cable stack for pushdowns and face pulls.

## Current status

- **Pick: Fitness SF Fillmore**, 1455 Fillmore, about 10 minutes away. Month-to-month, about $129/mo; the price isn't confirmed on their site.
- **Backups:** Live Fit Hayes Valley ($117/mo), and MX3 Lower Haight (private rack station, waitlisted).
- **Cheapest:** 24 Hour Fitness Van Ness, $25–50/mo, about 23 minutes away.
