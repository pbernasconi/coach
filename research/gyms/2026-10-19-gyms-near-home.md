# Open gyms near 945 Steiner St (updated 2026-10-19)

- **Home:** Steiner between Fulton and McAllister, on the north edge of Alamo Square.
- **What's included:** open gyms only, meaning you walk in and lift on your own. Class studios are left out.
- **Walk times:** my estimates from the street grid, not a routing app.
- **Prices:** from each gym's site or a recent listing. Confirm them before signing.

## Recommendation

**Fitness SF Fillmore.** It's the closest, at about 10 minutes. It has racks and platforms, stays open until midnight Mon–Thu, and has a pool, sauna and cold plunge. It's month-to-month with no sign-up fee.

- **Cheaper backup:** Live Fit Hayes Valley ($117/mo Basic, corrected from $107).
- **Closest of all:** MX3 Lower Haight, 520 Waller (~10 min). You book a private rack station. It's waitlisted right now.
- **Cheapest overall:** 24 Hour Fitness Van Ness, about 23 minutes away.

## Check on the Fitness SF day pass

Ask about the heaviest dumbbell, the cable stations (for rope pushdowns and face pulls), the EZ bar, the preacher bench and the dip station. Also confirm the monthly price.
